What athletes work on

Five things every sport needs

Team practice is mostly skills and games. U Can Train adds the athletic side, so players move better, get faster and stay healthy all season.

Mobility

Hips, ankles and shoulders that move freely.

Explosiveness

Jumps, bounds and a quicker first step.

Strength

Dumbbell and bodyweight work, form first.

Balance

Single-leg control and stability.

Injury Prevention

Hamstrings, groin and landing mechanics.

Know before you go

Your first Saturday

Wear

Athletic clothes and clean tennis shoes. No cleats or shoes worn outside on the studio floor.

Bring

A water bottle and a towel. All equipment is provided.

Arrive

5–10 minutes early so the session starts on time.

Parents

Parents are welcome to wait in the lobby. The training studio is for athletes and coaches only.

Can't Make It?

Cancel at least 24 hours ahead to move to another slot. Cancellations within 24 hours aren't refunded.

Injuries

Note any injuries at signup and tell a coach at check-in. Exercises can be adjusted.

Questions

FAQ

My athlete has never lifted. Is that okay?

Yes. Every exercise has a modified version, and coaches teach form before adding weight. Younger athletes mostly use bodyweight and light weights.

Is this affiliated with the school?

No. U Can Train runs independently at the U Can Danz! Dance Company studio. Attending has no effect on any school team, roster or playing time.

Will this conflict with club or school training?

No. It's designed to complement them, and coaches keep the load sensible for athletes who are in season.

How do you keep athletes safe?

Two coaches at every session, small groups, foam plyo boxes, and coaching on how to land and move. Safety is our first concern, and we keep a first-aid kit and emergency contacts on hand.

How does the 5-pack work?

$85 for five sessions, good on any Saturday within 10 weeks of purchase. Athletes new to U Can Train who sign up by Nov 6 pay $75.
